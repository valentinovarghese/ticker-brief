The asymmetry — same night, same excuse, opposite reactions

Amazon guided revenue below expectations and rose 9%. Apple beat and guided to 9–11% growth, and fell 6.6%. Both blamed supply. The difference is contracted demand. Amazon could point to booked 2027 capacity, so its shortfall reads as revenue deferred. Apple has no equivalent backlog to show, so its shortfall reads as revenue lost. That distinction, not the size of either beat, is what set the two reactions.

The asymmetry worth understanding

Microsoft and Meta both raised spending sharply on the same night, to opposite verdicts. The difference is not the amount — it is what stands behind it. Microsoft can point to $678B of signed contracts. Meta cannot point to any. Investors call the first investment and the second cost.
