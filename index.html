The asymmetry, same night, same excuse, opposite reactions

Amazon guided revenue below expectations and rose. Apple beat and guided to 9–11% growth, and fell. Both blamed the same memory shortage. The difference is what the extra cost buys. Amazon's higher spending buys revenue-generating capacity against a $496B booked backlog, so its shortfall reads as revenue deferred. Apple's higher cost buys nothing. It just compresses margin on a product whose price is already set, so its shortfall reads as revenue lost. That distinction, not the size of either beat, is what set the two reactions.

Read-through

The asymmetry worth understanding
